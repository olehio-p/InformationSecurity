Mathematical Requirements
For maximum period (m), the following is required:
  • When c = 0:
    • m must be a prime number
    • a must be a primitive root modulo m
    • Period = m - 1
  • When c ≠ 0:
    • GCD(c, m) = 1
    • a - 1 must be divisible by all prime factors of m
    • If m is divisible by 4, then a - 1 must also be divisible by 4
    • Period = m
Important Notes
What you need to know:
  • Seed (X₀):
    • Must be in the range [0, m)
    • Cannot be 0 if c = 0
    • Sequence starting point depends on the seed
  • Limitations:
    • Algorithm is deterministic
    • Same parameters yield the same sequence
    • Not suitable for cryptography
  • Recommendations:
    • Use large prime numbers for m
    • Test with a large number of values (10000+)
    • Compare with system random generator

Lehmer's Algorithm Theory

The Linear Congruential Generator (LCG) algorithm was proposed by D. H. Lehmer in 1951. Formula: Xn+1 = (a × Xn + c) mod m

Cesaro's Theorem: The probability that two randomly chosen integers are coprime is 6/π² ≈ 0.6079. This allows for statistical estimation of π by generating pairs of pseudorandom numbers and counting those with GCD = 1.

Euclidean Algorithm: Used to find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of two numbers: GCD(a, b) = GCD(b, a mod b) until b = 0.
